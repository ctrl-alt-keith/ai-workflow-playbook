# Product Status

> Status: canonical Playbook current-state surface
>
> Authority: records the current accepted status of the Product identities
> listed here
>
> Provenance: each status entry links to the human governance decision that
> established it

## Purpose And Ownership

This document answers: **What Product status is accepted now?** For every
identity listed here, its entry is the canonical answer to that question.

A linked Product-promotion decision has a different role. It records the human
governance event, authority claim, effective date, evidence basis,
consequences, and lineage that established a state. It does not replace this
current-state surface or require a reader to reconstruct current status from an
event chain.

## State And Decision Update Invariant

A future status transition must:

1. preserve every prior decision record without rewriting its historical
   decision, evidence boundary, authority claim, or effective date;
2. append a new, stably identified human decision provenance record;
3. update the affected current-state entry to the new accepted status,
   effective date, and establishing decision; and
4. make the new state and decision link to each other in the same reviewed
   Playbook change.

As between Playbook surfaces, the current-state entry controls the answer to
the current-status question. The linked decision controls the historical claim
about the governance event. A new human decision takes effect on its stated
effective date whether or not this surface has been updated. A discrepancy is
a documentation defect; it does not authorize an agent, repository,
implementation, validator, or runtime to infer or perform another transition.

Only a new explicit human Product governance decision can change accepted
Product status. Implementation, operation, evidence, a recommendation,
validation, documentation, repository adoption, or the passage of time cannot
change it by implication.

## Scope And Non-Consequences

This surface records accepted state only for identities explicitly listed
here. It is not a general Product registry and does not establish a complete
Product catalog.

This document does not promote or import the Product lifecycle, registry,
promotion threshold, or other proposed rules in the
[Candidate Architecture Foundation](architecture-foundation-candidate.md).
That document remains non-authoritative candidate material.
