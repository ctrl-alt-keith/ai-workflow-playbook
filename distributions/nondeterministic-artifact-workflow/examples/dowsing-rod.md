# Dowsing Rod — worked pilot evidence

This is an example from the CAK-300 pilot,
not a required photo recipe. The pilot used in-house product photographs, a
handle detail, and a spring-clover background as source files. Its scene
contract asked for copper dowsing rods centered on the sun-dappled background,
with shape and dimensions protected, lighting and shadows allowed to vary,
the source photo's white background removed, and a square 2050 × 2050 output
preferred.

Two generated candidate files were retained. The human preferred candidate
file `dowsing-rod-candidate-v2.png` for better handle fidelity and lighting.
Its 1254 × 1254 pixels were
resized and exported to a new 2050 × 2050 RGB PNG using a deterministic
Lanczos step, with no generative or semantic edit. The saved derivative was
read back and its dimensions and bytes verified. The human then gave final
approval to that exact derivative, which was moved to Accepted Masters. The
review note records its SHA-256 as
`ce47fbde564e4c6b10e5ff68d050db2f31f7ccb33ce8060f9728c642e69bdd4f`.
The generated candidate file remains a candidate; approval applies to the
finished derivative. The review note does not record a publication decision.

Evidence: CAK-300's saved `dowsing-rod.md` scene contract and
`dowsing-rod-review.md` review note. Those records support this one run's
choice, finishing, and approval. They do
not by themselves demonstrate repeated-trial consistency or document the
physical-product comparison called for by CAK-300. Those remain within the
owning pilot task, outside this package's claims.
